## 6 · A rule I'm writing to

Slovak declines names ("proti Barcelone"), but a username or a club name in our text is whatever the player typed, so it can't be declined. Every Slovak line keeps a name in the nominative: "Nový majiteľ: Martin", "Odovzdať klub hráčovi Martin", never "z Martina". And no line guesses a player's gender: Slovak past tense has it ("poslal" or "poslala"), and a username doesn't say, so those lines are built without a gendered verb ("Žiadosť o priateľstvo od: Martin"). The press and the commentary (club names in sentences) will follow the same rule, or carry the few case forms a line needs ([04](04-LANGUAGES.md) §2.3).
